# GestureCare Publications

Public documents and strategic insights advancing front-line healthcare and transparency within direct care systems.

## About This Repository

This repository serves as the public record for GestureCare's defensive publications, technical disclosures, and strategic documents. Our mission is to improve the quality, transparency, and accessibility of direct care -- particularly for elder and memory care populations -- through open innovation in AI-enabled healthcare technology.

By publishing these documents openly, we aim to:

- **Establish prior art** that keeps critical healthcare innovations in the public domain
- **Promote transparency** in how AI and sensor technologies can serve vulnerable populations
- **Invite collaboration** from researchers, clinicians, care workers, and technologists
- **Prevent patent enclosure** of concepts that should remain freely available for the benefit of all

## Areas of Development

Our publications address the intersection of AI, wearable technology, and direct care across several key domains:

- **Continuous Mental Health Monitoring** -- Wearable sensor systems and AI methods for detecting and tracking cognitive decline, depression, anxiety, and other conditions in elder care populations
- **Behavioral Insight Generation** -- Multi-modal sensor fusion, personalized baseline learning, and pattern recognition to surface actionable insights for care teams
- **Collaborative Care Coordination** -- Platforms connecting care workers, families, and healthcare providers through structured observations, shift intelligence, and FHIR-based interoperability
- **Privacy-Preserving Architecture** -- On-device AI processing, federated learning, and data minimization principles that protect the people we serve
- **Open Research Enablement** -- De-identified data infrastructure and open protocols that support academic research in digital biomarkers, intervention optimization, and care quality
